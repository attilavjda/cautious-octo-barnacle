# Mapping the proof space around the triple-count conjecture

This note records a *lemma-discovery* pass made on the library, as opposed to a further attack on
the conjecture itself.  The final goal (uniformity of the triple count `N(v₁,v₂) = 2^{2n-3}` for
all `k`) was deliberately frozen; instead the surrounding region was scanned for statements that
are (a) structurally natural, (b) reusable outside the Kasami setting, and (c) not yet stated
anywhere in the library.  Everything below is proved, with no `sorry` and no new axioms.

## How the candidates were selected

Four mechanically checkable smells were used, all of them visible in a formalised library and
none of them visible on paper:

1. **A private helper is a public lemma in disguise.**  `Reductions/Zcount.lean` contained two
   private coordinate-swap lemmas for the Kasami triple count, and `Transfer.lean` a private
   Frobenius-transport lemma.  All three are statements about an arbitrary subset of an arbitrary
   field of characteristic two; none of them uses anything about `Δ`.
2. **A statement provable in a lower layer but stated in a higher one.**  Same three lemmas: they
   were stated in the `Conjecture` layer although they belong to the `Field` layer.
3. **A missing mirror.**  The count was known to be invariant under `ρ ↦ 1 + ρ`; the companion
   `ρ ↦ ρ⁻¹`, and hence the whole `S₃`, was absent, and so was the Frobenius symmetry `ρ ↦ ρ²`.
   The conjecture is the *third* correlation of `S`; the *second* correlation was absent.
   A positivity criterion had no negativity dual.
4. **The same proof skeleton twice.**  The two coordinate-swap proofs were the same 15-line
   `card_nbij'` argument with permuted coordinates.

## The new statements

All of them are new to this library.  Their *literature* status is discussed at the end.

### 1. The symmetry group of the parameter (`KasamiConjecture/Field/TripleCount.lean`)

For a field `F` of characteristic two, a finite `D ⊆ F` and coefficients `(v₁, v₂)` put

    N_D(v₁, v₂) = #{(x,y,z) ∈ D³ : v₁ x + v₂ y + (v₁ + v₂) z = 0},   P_D(ρ) = N_D(1, ρ).

* `tripleCountOf_comm`, `tripleCountOf_add` — coordinate permutations permute the coefficients
  (the three coefficients sum to zero, which is exactly what characteristic two buys).
* `tripleCountOf_smul`, `tripleCountOf_eq_paramCount` — the count is a function of `ρ = v₂/v₁`.
* `paramCount_add_one`, `paramCount_inv`, `paramCount_add_one_div`, `paramCount_inv_add_one`,
  `paramCount_div_add_one` — invariance under the six-element orbit
  `ρ, 1+ρ, ρ⁻¹, (1+ρ)⁻¹, (1+ρ)/ρ, ρ/(1+ρ)`, i.e. under the anharmonic `S₃`.
* `tripleCountOf_image` — transport along **any** injective ring endomorphism `σ`:
  `N_{σD}(σv₁, σv₂) = N_D(v₁, v₂)`.  This single lemma subsumes both the old private
  Frobenius-transfer lemma and the squaring symmetry.
* `sq_injective_of_charTwo`, `paramCount_sq` — if `D` is stable under squaring then
  `P_D(ρ²) = P_D(ρ)`.
* `ParamStep`, `paramCount_of_reachable` — the count is constant along the orbits of the group
  generated by `ρ ↦ 1+ρ`, `ρ ↦ ρ⁻¹`, `ρ ↦ ρ²`.

### 2. The two degenerate parameters (`Field/TripleCount.lean`, `Conjecture/Symmetry.lean`)

* `paramCount_zero`, `paramCount_one` — at the excluded parameters the plane degenerates and
  `P_D(0) = P_D(1) = |D|²`; the two excluded parameters form one orbit of the group above.
* `tripleCount_param_zero`, `tripleCount_param_one`, `tripleCount_param_zero_ne` — for `Δ` this
  is `2^{2n-2}`, which is *twice* the conjectured uniform value, so the exclusion of `{0,1}` in
  the statement of the conjecture is necessary and the conjecture is not vacuously stated.

### 3. `Δ` is Frobenius-stable, and the conjecture reduces to orbit representatives
(`Conjecture/Symmetry.lean`)

* `deltaMap_sq`, `deltaSet_sq_stable` — `δ(b²) = δ(b)²`, hence `Δ² = Δ`.
* `tripleCount_param_inv`, `tripleCount_param_sq`, `tripleCount_param_orbit`,
  `tripleCount_param_of_reachable` — the Kasami count therefore has all of the symmetries above.
* `hasUniformTriples_of_orbit_reps` — **the conjecture only has to be checked on one parameter
  per orbit**: a set `R` meeting every orbit suffices.  Generic orbits have size `6n`
  (`S₃ × Frobenius`), so this is a `6n`-fold reduction of the parameter space.
  `exists_orbit_reps` shows the hypothesis is satisfiable, so the reduction is not vacuous.

### 4. The moment ladder: the even rung (`Field/SetCharSum.lean`, `Conjecture/Moments.lean`)

For `S_D(a) = ∑_{t ∈ D} χ(a t)`:

* `sum_setCharSum_mul` — `∑_λ S_D(λ) S_D(λρ) = 2^n · #{(x,y) ∈ D² : x + ρy = 0}`.
* `pairSolutionCount_eq_card_inter` — that count is `|D ∩ ρD|` for `ρ ≠ 0`.
* `sum_setCharSum_mul_ne_zero`, `sum_setCharSum_sq_ne_zero` — the same with the trivial term
  removed, and the second moment `2^n|D| − |D|²`.
* `sum_deltaCharSum_pair`, `sum_deltaCharSum_sq` — for `Δ`: the second rung is
  `2^n |Δ ∩ ρΔ| − 2^{2n-2}`, and the second moment is exactly `2^{2n-2}`.

  The point of the framing: the conjecture is the *odd* rung `∑_{λ≠0} S(λ)S(λρ)S(λ(1+ρ)) = 0` of
  a ladder whose even rungs are elementary and are governed by the intersection numbers of `Δ`
  with its dilates.

### 5. Sign criteria: mean-zero plus one-sided bound (`Conjecture/Moments.lean`)

`Z` was already known to have mean zero over the admissible parameters.  Hence:

* `hasUniformTriples_of_tripleCharSum_nonneg` — if `Z(ρ) ≥ 0` for all admissible `ρ`, the
  conjecture holds;
* `hasUniformTriples_of_tripleCharSum_nonpos` — the dual;
* `tripleCharSum_eq_sub`, `tripleCharSum_of_reachable`, `tripleCharSum_param_inv`,
  `tripleCharSum_param_sq`, `tripleCharSum_param_add_one` — `Z` inherits every symmetry of the
  count, so the sign question, too, need only be settled on one parameter per orbit.

This converts the conjecture from an *equality* problem into an *inequality* problem, which is
the shape (positivity + mean zero ⇒ vanishing) that the discovery pass was looking for.

## Deduplication achieved along the way

The three ad-hoc proofs that suggested the general statements were deleted and their call sites
re-pointed:

* `tripleCount_swap12` (private, and in fact never used) — deleted;
* `tripleCount_swap23` (private) — deleted, `tripleCount_one_add_one` is now a one-line
  corollary of `tripleCountOf_add`;
* `tripleCount_eq_tripleCount_one` — now a one-line corollary of `tripleCountOf_eq_paramCount`;
* `tripleCount_frobenius_transfer` — its 40-line bijection proof replaced by a 7-line
  specialisation of `tripleCountOf_image`.

Net effect on the pre-existing files: about 85 lines removed, no API lost.

## Honest status of the "newness" claim

*New to this library*: all of the statements above; each is proved here from scratch.

*New to the literature*: not claimed.  This region — Kasami exponents, difference sets of APN
power maps, Weil sums over `GF(2^n)` — has a dense folklore, and statements such as "the number
of solutions of `x + ρy + (1+ρ)z = 0` in a set is invariant under the anharmonic group" or
"Parseval for a subset" are the kind of thing that is used without citation whenever it is
needed.  What is defensible is the weaker and more useful claim: these are the statements the
region *needs*, at the generality at which they are true, machine-checked, and now available for
reuse — and two of them (`hasUniformTriples_of_orbit_reps`, the sign criteria) are genuine
reductions of the open problem rather than restatements of it.
